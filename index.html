Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AI business automation

What is AI business automation?

AI business automation uses artificial intelligence to handle repetitive business tasks like answering phones, posting on social media, and capturing leads. Blue Oak Automation provides three core engines: Phone Intelligence (24/7 AI call handling), Content Intelligence (social media automation), and Lead Intelligence (automated lead capture and nurturing).

How much does Blue Oak Automation cost?

Blue Oak Automation offers flexible pricing from $697/month per engine for standard service, or Community Impact pricing starting at $397/month for qualifying small businesses. Our Complete Automation Suite (all three engines) is $1,697/month. All plans include no contracts and the ability to cancel anytime.

Does the AI phone assistant work 24/7?

Yes. Blue Oak's Phone Intelligence Engine operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. The AI assistant answers calls instantly, captures caller information, books appointments, and provides information to customers at any time of day or night.

Do I need a contract to use Blue Oak Automation?

No. Blue Oak Automation operates on a month-to-month basis with no long-term contracts required. You can cancel anytime without penalties or fees. We earn your business every month through results and service.

What areas does Blue Oak Automation serve?

Blue Oak Automation serves small businesses in Detroit, Michigan, and throughout Jamaica. We're the first AI automation company operating in Jamaica and specialize in serving the 47,000+ small businesses in the Detroit metro area.

How quickly can I get started with Blue Oak Automation?

Most businesses are fully operational within 5-7 business days. After your free consultation, we'll set up your AI systems, integrate them with your existing tools, and provide training. You can start seeing results immediately after launch.

What makes Blue Oak different from other automation companies?

Blue Oak Automation offers enterprise-grade AI at small business prices with no contracts, transparent pricing, and dedicated support. Unlike competitors requiring $10,000+ upfront and annual contracts, we provide month-to-month service starting at $397-$697 per engine with the flexibility to cancel anytime.

Can I try the AI phone assistant before committing?

Yes! Call our AI assistant Maya at +1 (248) 834-9808 to experience the technology firsthand. Maya is available 24/7 and can answer questions about our services. We also offer a free business health audit to show you exactly how much revenue you're losing to missed opportunities.

Will the AI phone assistant sound robotic?

No. Blue Oak's AI phone assistant uses advanced natural language processing to sound conversational and professional. Most callers don't realize they're speaking with AI. The system can handle complex questions, transfer calls when needed, and provide a seamless customer experience.

How does social media automation work?

Blue Oak's Content Intelligence Engine creates, schedules, and posts content to your social media channels automatically. The AI analyzes your industry, creates relevant posts, and maintains consistent engagement with your audience. You maintain full approval control over all content before it's published.
